Logistics Coordinator (m/f/d) – Hamburg Port

About us
Northwind Freight moves containers between Hamburg, Rotterdam and Gdansk. Our dispatch team of 14 plans road and rail transport for 300 customers.

Your tasks
- Plan and book container transport by truck and rail
- Prepare customs documents (ATLAS, T1, EX-A) and check invoices
- Track shipments in our TMS and update customers by phone and e-mail
- Coordinate with hauliers, terminals and the customs broker
- Solve delays and damage claims

Requirements:
- Completed apprenticeship as freight forwarding agent (Speditionskaufmann) or similar
- 2+ years in freight forwarding or logistics coordination
- Customs clearance knowledge (ATLAS, Incoterms 2020)
- Confident with SAP TM or another TMS, and with Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP)
- Very good English and German, Polish is a plus
- Self-motivated, structured, calm under pressure

What we offer
30 days of holiday, a job ticket and a modern office at the harbour.
