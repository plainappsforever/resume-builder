Digital Marketing Manager
Fernway Outdoor Gear, Denver (hybrid)

Fernway sells hiking and camping gear online and in 12 stores. We are looking for a Digital Marketing Manager who turns data into campaigns that bring new customers to our e-commerce shop.

In this role you will:
* Plan and run paid campaigns on Google Ads and Meta Ads with a monthly budget of $80k
* Own SEO for our online shop together with our content team
* Build email marketing journeys and newsletters in our marketing automation tool
* Report on KPIs such as ROI, conversion rate and customer acquisition cost
* Run A/B testing on landing pages and email subject lines
* Brief agencies and freelancers on creative work

Requirements:
* 4+ years in digital marketing or performance marketing, ideally e-commerce
* Hands-on experience with Google Ads, Meta Ads and Google Analytics
* Good knowledge of SEO and content marketing
* Experience with email marketing and marketing automation (e.g. HubSpot or Mailchimp)
* Analytical mindset: you know your way around Excel and dashboards
* Strong copywriting skills in English

Nice to have:
* Social media marketing and influencer marketing experience
* Love for the outdoors

We offer a gear allowance, flexible hours and a friendly team.
