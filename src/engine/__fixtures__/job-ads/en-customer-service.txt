Customer Service Representative
BrightLoop Internet, remote (US time zones)

BrightLoop provides fiber internet to 300,000 homes. Our customer service team answers calls, chats and emails and fixes problems on the first contact whenever possible.

Responsibilities:
- Answer inbound calls, live chat and email support requests
- Troubleshoot connection problems with customers step by step
- Document every case in our ticketing system (Zendesk)
- Handle complaints and escalations calmly and follow up until resolved
- Meet our service level agreements and customer satisfaction goals
- Suggest improvements to our help center articles

Requirements:
- 2+ years in customer service, customer support or a call center
- Excellent written and verbal communication in English
- Experience with Zendesk or another ticketing system
- Basic technical troubleshooting skills for routers and Wi-Fi
- Calm under pressure, with active listening and de-escalation skills
- Quiet home workspace and reliable internet

Nice to have:
- Bilingual English and Spanish
- Experience with CRM software such as Salesforce

We offer paid training, health insurance and a home office allowance.
