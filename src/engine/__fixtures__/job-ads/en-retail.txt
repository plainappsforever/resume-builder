Retail Sales Associate (Part-Time)
Copper Kettle Home Store, Portland OR

Copper Kettle is a family-owned home and kitchen store. We help customers find the right pan, knife or gift, and we know our products well.

What you will do:
• Greet customers and give friendly product advice
• Work the cash register and handle cash, cards and returns at the point of sale
• Restock shelves, keep displays tidy and support visual merchandising
• Meet weekly sales goals together with the store team
• Help with inventory counts and receiving deliveries
• Open or close the store twice a week

Requirements:
• 1 year of retail sales or customer service experience
• Cash handling experience and comfort with POS systems
• Friendly, patient manner with customers
• Product knowledge of kitchen tools is a plus, or the wish to learn it
• Available on weekends and some evenings
• Basic computer skills

Nice to have:
• Visual merchandising experience
• Spanish speaking

Why us: employee discount, flexible scheduling and paid training.
