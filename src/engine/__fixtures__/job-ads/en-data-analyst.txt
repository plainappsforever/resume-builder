Data Analyst
Tallow & Pine Insurance, Chicago IL (hybrid)

Tallow & Pine is a mid-size insurer. The analytics team answers questions from claims, pricing and marketing, and we are hiring a Data Analyst to help us move from spreadsheets to reliable dashboards.

What you'll do:
• Write SQL queries against our Snowflake data warehouse
• Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau and Power BI
• Clean and model data with Python (pandas) and dbt
• Run statistical analysis and A/B testing for marketing campaigns
• Present findings to stakeholders in clear, short reports
• Improve data quality checks with the data engineering team

Requirements:
• 2+ years as a data analyst or in business intelligence
• Advanced SQL and Excel (pivot tables, lookups)
• Experience with Tableau or Power BI
• Python for data analysis (pandas, Jupyter)
• Good grasp of statistics and regression analysis
• Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have:
• Experience with dbt or Airflow
• Insurance or finance background

Benefits: hybrid work, 401(k) match, training budget.
