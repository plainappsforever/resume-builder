Warehouse Associate – Night Shift
Harbor Point Fulfillment · Reno, NV

About the role
Harbor Point Fulfillment ships home goods for online stores across the West Coast. Our night shift keeps orders moving so customers get their parcels the next morning. You will join a crew of 30 associates working in a 200,000 sq ft facility.

What you'll do:
• Pick and pack customer orders accurately using RF scanners
• Operate a forklift and reach truck to move pallets between racking and the dock
• Handle shipping and receiving, including unloading trucks and checking deliveries against purchase orders
• Run cycle counts and report inventory discrepancies in our WMS
• Keep aisles clean and follow our 5S and workplace safety routines

Requirements:
• 1+ year of warehouse experience in order fulfillment or distribution
• Forklift certification (or willingness to earn it in your first 30 days)
• Comfortable with RF scanners and a warehouse management system
• Able to lift 50 lbs and stand for a 10-hour shift
• Basic math for inventory counts; attention to detail
• Reliable attendance and good teamwork on a busy shift

Nice to have:
• Experience with SAP EWM or another WMS
• OSHA 10 safety training
• Reach truck experience

What we offer:
Night shift differential, weekly pay, paid time off, and on-site training on every piece of equipment.
