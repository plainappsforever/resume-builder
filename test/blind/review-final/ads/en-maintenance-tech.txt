Maintenance Technician – 2nd Shift
Riverbend Packaging Co. · Dayton, OH · Full-time

About us
Riverbend Packaging runs four high-speed corrugated lines and a folding-carton department. We are a family-owned plant with 180 employees.

What you'll do
- Troubleshoot and repair electrical, mechanical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems on production equipment
- Perform preventive maintenance according to the CMMS schedule and close work orders in Maximo
- Read electrical schematics and wiring diagrams; work on 480V three-phase motors and VFDs
- Diagnose PLC faults (Allen-Bradley ControlLogix, RSLogix 5000) together with the controls engineer
- Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) and OSHA safety procedures at all times
- Fabricate and weld simple brackets and guards (MIG welding)
- Document root cause analysis for repeated breakdowns

Requirements
- 3+ years of industrial maintenance experience in a manufacturing environment
- Associate degree in industrial maintenance or equivalent journeyman certification
- Working knowledge of PLC troubleshooting and motor controls
- Hydraulics and pneumatics troubleshooting
- Comfortable using hand tools, multimeter and laser alignment tools
- Forklift certification is a plus
- Must be able to lift 50 lbs and work overtime when needed

What we offer
Medical, dental and vision insurance from day one, 401(k) with 5% match, shift differential, tool allowance, paid training.

Riverbend Packaging is an equal opportunity employer.
