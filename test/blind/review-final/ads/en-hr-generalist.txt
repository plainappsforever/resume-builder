HR Generalist (Hybrid)
Northline Health Partners — Denver, CO

Northline Health Partners operates 14 outpatient clinics across Colorado. Our People team supports 900 employees.

Your role
You will be the first point of contact for managers and employees in our Denver and Boulder clinics. You will:
• Manage the full employee lifecycle: onboarding, offboarding, transfers and leave of absence cases (FMLA, ADA)
• Advise managers on employee relations issues, conduct investigations and document outcomes
• Maintain employee data in Workday and produce monthly headcount and turnover reports
• Support open enrollment and answer benefits administration questions
• Coordinate the annual performance review cycle and compensation review
• Ensure compliance with federal and Colorado employment law; update the employee handbook
• Partner with Talent Acquisition on job descriptions and offer letters

Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field
3–5 years of experience as an HR Generalist, ideally in healthcare
Solid knowledge of FMLA, ADA, FLSA and EEO regulations
Experience with an HRIS (Workday preferred) and advanced Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP)
SHRM-CP or PHR certification preferred
Excellent written communication and discretion with confidential information

Benefits
Hybrid schedule (3 days on site), 22 days PTO, tuition reimbursement, 403(b) with match.
